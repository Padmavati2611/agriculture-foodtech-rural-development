The Challenge

A Cold Chain Gap in India's Food Bowl

The North Eastern Region grows abundant fresh produce, yet a broken cold chain forces farmers to sell at throwaway prices — or watch their harvest rot.

01

Heavy Post-Harvest Losses

Roughly 15–20% of fresh vegetables from the NER are lost between harvest and consumption — far above the national average — due to high humidity, perishability and long, hilly transit routes.

02

No Grid, No Cold Storage

Many NER villages lack reliable grid power. Conventional refrigerated warehouses are few, distant, diesel-dependent and expensive, leaving smallholders with no affordable storage option near the farm.

03

Distress Selling & Price Volatility

Without storage, farmers must sell immediately, often fetching only 30–40% of retail value. Unable to wait for better prices, they lose bargaining power to middlemen.

04

Wasted Energy & Resources

Every kg of produce wasted also wastes the water, fertiliser, labour and fuel used to grow and transport it — a drain on rural livelihoods and the environment alike.

⚠️

Combined, these gaps mean billions of rupees of potential farmer income is lost in the NER every year, while urban consumers pay premium prices for imported veggies.

Our Solution

SunChill — Cold Storage That Runs on Sunshine

A portable, solar-powered smart cold room designed for NER's terrain, climate and farmer realities.

What is SunChill?

SunChill is a decentralised mini cold storage unit built on insulated panels, powered entirely by rooftop solar + battery, and governed by an AI-assisted smart controller. It maintains optimum temperature and humidity for different vegetables — extending shelf life from days to weeks right at the farm gate.

  • 2–5 tonne capacity — right-sized for smallholder clusters & producer groups (FPOs).
  • 100% solar, off-grid — works even where the grid cannot reach.
  • IoT remote monitoring — farmers & buyers see live status on a mobile app.
  • Modular & portable — can be deployed in the most remote districts.
  • Walk-in or multi-room — separate zones for leafy greens, tubers and fruit.

Technical Design

System Architecture

From sun to sensor — the flow of power and data inside SunChill.

⚡ Power & Cooling

  • Solar PV array — 5.0–7.5 kWp, east-west mount
  • MPPT charge controller + hybrid inverter
  • LiFePO4 battery bank — 48V, ~20 kWh
  • Variable-speed DC compressor on R290 refrigerant
  • PCM cold-buffer panels (zero-credit shifted load)

📡 Sensing & Control

  • IoT controller (ESP32-class) + edge firmware
  • Temp & humidity probes (DS18B20 / SHT31)
  • Door-open, power & airflow sensors
  • PID control loop optimises compressor duty
  • Local micro-SD data logging for offline periods

🌐 Connectivity & Cloud

  • Cellular (2G/4G) + optional LoRa mesh for valleys
  • Cloud backend — live telemetry & analytics
  • Farmer mobile app (Hindi/English/NER languages)
  • Predictive alerts for batch ripening & power events
  • Admin dashboard for FPO/cooperative managers

Under the Hood

How SunChill Uses Location, IoT and AI

Five simple layers, each with a distinct job — together they protect the harvest.

📍

Location

Identifies where the SunChill unit is operating and provides local environmental conditions.

🌬️

Weather

Provides outdoor temperature and humidity based on the selected location — so the unit knows its ambient heat load.

📡

IoT

Measures the actual temperature and humidity INSIDE the cold-storage unit using physical sensors.

🧠

AI

Uses sensor and storage data to identify abnormal conditions and estimate spoilage risk.

🚨

Alerts

Warns the farmer when a critical condition — temperature, battery or door — requires attention.
